This study is a **synthetic seeded-policy game**, not an assertion of production unpredictability or cryptographic audit safety. The genuine exact-value target witness is an assumed additional authenticated information channel.

## Scientific question

Does a mixture of the seven existing k=16 auditors, fitted to the **historical NOR-26 training cross-play only**, defend against prospective attacks that optimize exposure to the mixture better than the frozen hybrid_50 policy? How much synthetic known-template detection is sacrificed to improve worst-case attack coverage?

The hypothesis may fail. Preserve the original numerical answer and the raw source-level negatives. In particular, maximizing worst-case detection may favor pure uniform exploration rather than any useful learned mixture.
