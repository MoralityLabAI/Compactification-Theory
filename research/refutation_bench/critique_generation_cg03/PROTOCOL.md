# RB-CG-03: conditional entailment critiques, countermodels and premise repair

## Motivation
CG-02's 789-parameter executor and zero-parameter binding control both saturated its closed syntax task. CG-01's raw-source decoder failed depth transfer. CG-03 moves the hard work to semantic search under a budget: the original Lean theorem is valid, but its advertised statement may omit essential hypotheses.

## Registered source families
192 independent planted CNF families: 96 train (seed 103301), 24 calibration (103302), 36 deep (103303), 36 clutter transfer (103304). Four arms per family: original hypotheses, a valid omission of only decoys, and two omissions each removing a different essential core clause plus decoys. Train chain length 2–4; depth 5–6; clutter split length 2–4 with 30–48 planted decoy clauses versus 8–16 in train/cal/deep. Each chain has a unit start, two three-literal clauses per transition encoding implication with opposite auxiliary literals, and a positive goal. Variable permutation and clause order randomized. The source and both weakened countermodel valuations satisfy all decoys by construction. Each erroneous advertisement is false under its retained hypotheses. Candidate repairs are precisely the omitted original hypotheses; more than one restoring clause may be valid. A critique must distinguish an omitted essential premise from a harmless omission. All arms of each family stay together.

Native Lean 4.19.0: compile the original theorem, harmless-omission theorem, repaired claims using an explicit propositional resolution lemma, and Boolean counterexample witnesses for the false advertised statements. These are finite synthetic theorem families, not general Lean proof extraction. The source frontend supplies the literal clauses and selected hypotheses corresponding to the theorem types, not the reference proof bodies or planted assignments.

## Output and exact checking
SUPPORTED requires an independently checked DPLL refutation of retained hypotheses plus negated goal. REFUTED requires a total Boolean valuation satisfying every retained clause and falsifying the goal. A REPAIR finding additionally names an omitted clause and provides a checked refutation after adding it. The original source is satisfiable, hence adding any subset of its hypotheses preserves satisfiability. An unresolved attempt returns ABSTAIN; a proved refutation may be returned without a repair. No rejected certificate is promoted into a critique. Report scope: an advertised inference can fail while the source theorem remains valid.

DPLL certificate grammar: a conflict leaf or a split on a previously unassigned variable with two child certificates. The checker independently replays unit propagation at each node; conflict leaves must close. Total valuations are checked directly. An accepted repair proof uses exactly the advertised clauses, its named additional clause and negated goal. No target or gold metadata enters the policy. Model-generated outputs are structured mathematical critiques; prose can be rendered deterministically from them.

## Search and controls
Charge one node per DPLL call (including calls closed by propagation), shared across initial entailment/counterexample search and repair trials. Stop at 4/8/16/32/64/128 nodes. Count actual nodes and propagation scans separately; this is not a matched CPU-time test. Once SAT is found, rank omitted clauses falsified by the witness (a necessary condition for a one-clause repair). Attempt ranked repairs using the remaining budget. A completed repair must pass the independent checker; retain initial countermodels even if repair fails. Compare fixed lexicographic, occurrence, Jeroslow–Wang, and a small learned branch scorer (two seeds 81/83) and a trained repair scorer. For learned and symbolic policies use identical DPLL and certificate checker.

Training targets use exact bitset truth masks on train families only: at visited residual states, candidate literals are labeled by existence of a consistent countermodel; among satisfiable candidates prefer larger residual model count, and for unsatisfiable states prefer smaller unresolved-clause count after unit propagation. Train a 32-hidden-unit scorer on pairwise action ranking (soft target over all tied best actions), 12 epochs, AdamW .003, batch 64; fixed seeds. Repair scorer trains on all omitted candidates using exact entailment of the one-clause repaired case; use raw clause features, observed countermodel and remaining clause statistics. Test actor never sees model counts or truth masks. Train on states collected from fixed deterministic search; no held-out checkpoint selection.

Primary gate: at 16 and 32 nodes combined on depth and clutter splits, both learned seeds must exceed best fixed symbolic policy's number of complete critique certificates (support or refutation+repair) by at least 8, with zero accepted invalid certificates. Secondary report basic verdict, repair coverage, proposed rejected certificates, cost, and false accusation. Full exact bitset oracle is correctness ceiling, not budget-matched. A failed gate is preserved. Mechanism transfer remains constrained to these planted CNFs, with no claim of arbitrary mathematical critique or natural-language generation.
