# RB-CG-05: original Lean theorem correspondence audit (native development pilot)

## Purpose
The CG-01 through CG-04 synthetic track established a working certificate verifier and explored small learned acquisition and repair policies. This track returns to original mathematical source, including MathCan's RH interface and window lemmas. The selected source is the unchanged public MathCan commit 6d02b611c7358216379cb36952512a104e0bdb8e, Lean 4.35.0. The source is genuine; the misleading advertisements are newly authored counterfactual variants and are not allegations against the original authors, MathCan, or the classical RH proof. All modules and labels are public development data.

## Case contract
For each paired case provide raw original module source, selected theorem fully qualified name, and the independently stated advertised Lean proposition. The trusted basis is propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound. A selected declaration may be a correct conditional theorem while an advertisement deletes a premise or strengthens its conclusion. Positive certificates explicitly apply the selected source theorem to the advertised type. Negative certificates prove the negation of the advertised universal theorem, usually by applying it to a concrete witness satisfying every retained premise. The original declaration itself must compile and pass its axiom audit on both arms.

Each source module is compiled directly into an isolated module cache. Each certificate is then independently compiled in a separate file importing that original module; require no admissions and an allowed transitive axiom set for both original declaration and certificate. Exact source and certificate hashes, logs and raw input-only prompts archived. No gold certificates, mechanism labels, explanatory audit paraphrases or verdicts in exported prompts. Gold is separately exported. The two arms stay together. All cases in this pilot are dev; a larger prospective training/test source-family split is required before a learned transfer claim. No model training claimed here.

## Registered case families
- RHWindowExponents: log floor upper-window premise, four error terms third bound, normalized character error domination.
- RHMainLogic: missing RecTExists field, missing final RH bridge, legitimate removal of an unused literature argument versus dropping the proof of P.
- FiniteHorizonGap: missing length bound on second word, one-sixth clearance premise.
- NOR19StreamedVerifier: strict materialization improvement at zero length, equality inferred from mere equal lengths.
- PhaseLatch: strict near-future result without displacement comparison.
- AuthorizationQuotient: strengthening at-least-one error to both errors.

12 paired families, 24 cases. All source modules import Init only. The original abstractions are audited at their stated types; a criticism of a synthetic advertisement is not a refutation of a source theorem.

## Later learning plan
A small recurrent critic should emit typed critique obligations (apply theorem, instantiate witness, provide retained hypotheses, discharge contradiction), using a bounded native verifier. Baselines include exact statement comparison, native tactic search and bounded finite instantiation. Record proof terms, certificates, source and goal states, calls, wall time, unsupported proposals, and abstention. Add prospective original modules and hold out full source families rather than splitting edits of the same theorem. Free-form prose still requires independent semantic review.
