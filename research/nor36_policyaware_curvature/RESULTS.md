## Registered question

NOR-35 showed +16.73 percentage points of high-curvature attack coverage when the auditor moves half of every old risk-atom quota onto the public highest-curvature positions, but with a −2.14 point loss on bottom-curvature attacks. NOR-36 lets an attacker with PUBLIC policy knowledge optimize four changed target-output coordinate locations over ALL m positions using **256 calibration audit schedules and eight matched greedy restarts**. The attack is frozen before **512 independently salted evaluation schedules**. The outcome measures how much of NOR35's feature-aligned gain survives a learned response to the public deterministic inspection prefix.

Six equal-budget defender distributions use k=16 authenticated target-output coefficient comparisons on every audit: original_h50, atom_random_h50, atom_curv_h50, uniform16, original_mix and atom_curv_mix. Each mixture uses the original frozen NOR31 integer [2,0,1,1,4,8]/16 weighting of six NOR25 component policies. The curvature50 and atom-random transforms preserve exactly each original policy's **eight tail/top-magnitude/lag atom inspection quotas t_j** on every source/trial. Every defender-specific attacker receives the SAME greedy search budget, including mixture-aware weighted calibration exposure minimization. Each starts from eight SHA-seeded tie orders, greedily selects four distinct positions from the entire future, and receives no target-derived honest witness information.

The study uses **24 fresh public-only RRC25 sources**, replicates 52–55 × observed degree 512/1024 × three wide families. All source public-input SHA identities are distinct and excluded from **132** original NOR26/30/31/32/33/34/35 public inputs. Complete public accepted-program mask and hypothetical full query continuation freeze before original private generating programs and true outputs are reconstructed. The authentic external target-coefficient witness and genuinely independent private audit coins are ASSUMPTIONS.

The **exact theoretical average** for a truly uniform four-position attack across all m positions is the same for ANY fixed k=16 audit plan: 1−C(m−16,4)/C(m,4), as proved in NOR32. Differences among the observed *eight sampled whole-output attacks/source* are finite fixed-attack sampling effects and should not be interpreted as a source-independent uniform attack advantage. Likewise, the theorem says eight-atom quota-matched attack detection is identical across quota-preserving transforms **after integrating over all conditional altered-position sets**. The eight actual sampled matched attacks can have different finite-sample frequencies.
