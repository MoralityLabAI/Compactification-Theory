The BPD native-positive toy trains one pair of shared read/write matrices for all four synthetic passes. Through a **hand-designed** channel-swap transition T and readout C, its component observability matrix `O_k=stack(C T^j U^T, j=0..k)` changes rank from 1 to 2 in eight of eight fresh trained-factor seeds. A component donor swap recovers the donor future scalar output in 512/512 tests, and independent norm-matched random fiber patches succeed 54/512. The donor target is defined from the BPD component write and the observer is designed to expose the hidden axis. Thus this validates the delayed-observability test instrument and its native model implementation.

SCM-21 is a fundamentally different phenomenon: its recurrence is a trained 128-hidden four-visit model, its readout is an actual 64-program task output, and native learned VPD writes on 768 common-eligible recipient/donor tests recover **0** donor answers. The rank-four difference-PCA arm transfers 147/768, while head-readable difference transfers 413/768, but its patch doses differ greatly from the native-write doses. The task, distribution, representation and patch semantics do not match the BPD positive toy. Comparing 512/512 to 0/768 as a causal treatment effect is therefore invalid.

For the next learned-recurrence BPD test, freeze trained component factors, a task-output readout and the actual per-visit state-transition Jacobians on calibration tasks only. For a component state-write span U and readout q, compute the restricted delayed-response Gramian `sum_{j=1}^k U J(q o F^j)^T J(q o F^j) U^T`. Then test whether predicted invisible-at-now/delayed-visible directions change a named independently verified variable after native same-norm patches, matching random and task-agnostic subspaces. Control first for immediate logit parity, clip perturbation doses, and evaluate independent task roots and seeds with EvalMath power/variation thresholds. Preserve ARG-03-N sealed test. Only successful causal interchange on actual learned dynamics can promote the CQ-02 mathematical observation to neural mechinterp evidence.
