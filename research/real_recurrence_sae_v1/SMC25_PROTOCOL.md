## Question

SMC-24 predicted a true-correct-digit task-gradient vector from 48D hidden states and failed the original eight-cell gate. The correct target digit determines which task-margin functional was being predicted. This new experiment computes a **native full-logit directional tangent and all nine possible correct-digit margin responses** before consulting the ground-truth digit. It then asks whether an old-calibration-frozen probability distribution over digits can combine these candidate effects into a better estimate of the actual hard-sham-versus-feature-kill native margin response than matched-compute alternative combinations.

It tests the independent B501/B701 frozen 44,001-parameter recurrent teachers, with SAE seeds 71 and 89 on each. Teacher weights, SAE fits, source generator and the original BPD/SMC results remain untouched. The two teacher seeds are the independent model units; 128 puzzle sources are the within-model sample units.

## Scientific boundary

If the registered gate succeeds, a class-distribution-weighted candidate causal-effect estimate carries predictive information about *the true-digit finite physical erasure effect* beyond matched-computation uniform, top-label and scrambled-probability baselines on held-out Sudoku puzzle sources. This is an input-output causal-response prediction, not identification of semantic features, endogenous necessity or a globally invariant quotient. If it fails, class conditioning and temperature calibration under this fixed physical dose do not rescue the effect prediction on these weak independent teachers. No SMC-24 or SMC-23 scientific verdict is revised, and none of the sealed ARG-03-N 168 test cases will be accessed.
