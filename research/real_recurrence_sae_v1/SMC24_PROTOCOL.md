# SMC-24: Can a frozen gradient predictor explain target-native SAE erasure?

**Prospective registration, 17 November 2026, before new SMC-24 Sudoku data.** Follows the merged [SMC-23 negative result](SMC23_RESULTS.md) and [BPD-OUT03 exact gradient split](https://github.com/MoralityLabAI/BPD/blob/main/mechinterp/dark_fiber_gyms/SMC23_GRADIENT_SPLIT.md). The newly specified intervention source, calibration, frozen predictors, fresh puzzle seeds, and all decision gates are fixed here before training or test runs. All previous protocols and heldout cohorts remain unchanged.

## Main question

For an active SAE coordinate, its minimum-norm erasure delta and a same-dose hard sham s obey the first-order identity D_approx = g dot (delta-s), where g is the *native correct-digit margin gradient*. SMC-23 measured a negative or weak mean D in the independent B501 and B701 teachers but a strong positive effect in the considerably more capable pretrained A. Does a **label-free-on-test** prediction of g from the *clean physical hidden state* predict finite native margin differences on unseen Sudoku puzzles better than equally calibrated controls?

The primary teacher pair is B501 and B701, trained **independently** from scratch under the frozen SMC-13 regimen with broadly similar 40–50% heldout missing-cell competence. No new teacher is trained in SMC-24. Their two SAE71/89 fits per teacher are correlated measurements; there are exactly two independent teacher seeds. Source pretrained A can be reported only as a subsequent separate descriptive control, if run with its own preregistration. This study evaluates calibrated predictivity, not whether an active feature is beneficial. A negative effect can be predictable.

Preserve all 8 token slots even when ineligible.

## Statistics and registered acceptance

For each of 4 B-teacher/SAE fits × 2 fresh cohorts = **8 cells**, compute admitted per-goal absolute prediction error |D_true-D_pred| and paired MAE gain of trained ridge versus each of constant-gradient, goal-slot-shuffled-gradient ridge, and identically zero prediction. Aggregate per-puzzle mean gains over eligible goals within the puzzle. Resample all 64 puzzle IDs in 2,000 deterministic bootstrap draws, with **entire eight-goal clusters** preserved. Cells require ≥60 admitted goal observations across ≥30 independent puzzles and ≥1,990 valid bootstrap resamples, else label UNDERPOWERED.

Report 95% descriptive cluster intervals and two-sided familywise 99.375% Bonferroni intervals for the three registered MAE gain contrasts. The per-cell gate requires adjusted lower bound >0 on **all three** gains, plus ridge sign agreement with D_true over nonzero outcomes strictly higher than the constant-gradient baseline. Each teacher/SAE pair must pass on both novel cohorts, and program-level success requires at least one of the two SAE fits on each of B501 and B701 to pass both cohorts. Report all eight results without selecting favorable seeds or cohorts. Sign concordance is evaluated on the identical eligible population with predictions defined before native scoring; zero predicted sign counts as incorrect.

As a **post-scoring descriptive diagnosis only**, the scoring job may compute the exact goal-level native margin gradient g using the true test digit and clean-rival label, to estimate the finite Taylor residual |D_true-g dot (delta-s)|. This label-dependent oracle cannot enter any predicted primary score or gate and may be omitted for compute. Likewise an error-conditioned feature ranking or best checkpoint after test is exploratory only.

## Interpretation

Success would establish a calibration-frozen label-free **predictive association** between native hidden state and feature-erasure task-margin effects across independent puzzle contexts. It would not establish intrinsic sparse feature meaning, feature necessity, calibration-free self-understanding or a transfer from weaker to pretrained A. Failure may indicate the model's task gradient is too dependent on hidden true-digit labels to be predicted from this small calibration set, or that finite-dose nonlinear effects dominate. Original SMC-23 0/4 gate, BPD-OUT03 theorem, SMC-16/17 stacked results, and sealed 168-case ARG-03-N test remain unchanged.
