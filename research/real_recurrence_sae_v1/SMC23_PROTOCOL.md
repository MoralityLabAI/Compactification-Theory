# SMC-23: Target-native SAE selective erasure with source-independent feature choice

**Prospective registration, 17 November 2026.** This protocol is committed before any SMC-23 calibration, pilot, or fresh test output is computed. It follows the published SMC-14/15 work and the existing stacked [SMC-16](https://github.com/MoralityLabAI/Compactification-Theory/pull/177) and [SMC-17](https://github.com/MoralityLabAI/Compactification-Theory/pull/179) experiments. SMC-16 previously passed its source-mapped feature kill gate for 3/4 SAE pairs; its stronger inactive-preactivation matched SMC-17 control retained 1/4. Those older tests stay unchanged.

## Scientific objective

On frozen independent 44,001-parameter recurrent Sudoku teachers B501/B701, select each receiving teacher's naturally **highest-active SAE feature directly** and test whether selective suppression causes a greater loss of correct-digit margin than a physical equal-dose, outcome-blind, inactive-preactivation-matched tangent sham. Source A SAE71 is a separate capability comparator. Source-A Hungarian correspondences are verified for provenance but are never part of eligibility or target-feature choice.

## Frozen target-native selection and selective encoder kill

Choose exactly eight evenly spaced **visible blank** goals by SMC-14 goal_positions, independently of digit labels. On each goal choose the SAE's own strictly highest positive top-four feature j from its frozen FP32 encoder. Require exactly four positive top-four coordinates with a unique strongest feature. The physical encoder covectors are a_i=SAE.encoder.weight[i] / scale and decoder columns are d_i=scale*SAE.decoder.weight[:,i].

Let O contain the three other active encoder covectors and let v be the orthogonal projection of a_j onto ker(O), abstaining for rank defect or ||v|| <=1e-8. Set tau=0.02, delta=(z_j+tau)*v / ||v||², and inject h-delta. This is the affine-cell minimum-norm physical edit that suppresses the selected coordinate while preserving its other three active preactivations. Require ||delta|| <= 6*z_j*||d_j||. Reencode before inspecting any Sudoku outcome: target j must be zero, while each of the other three original active coefficients remains positive with relative drift at most 1e-4*(1+z_i). Record every failed gate and changed inactive feature.

For the fixed sham, compute the 44D nullspace of all four originally active physical encoder covectors. Offer the least-squares candidate minimizing change in 92 originally inactive preactivations relative to delta, plus exactly 32 seeded Gaussian nullspace directions. Normalize each candidate to the EXACT physical dose ||delta||, require reencoding to preserve all original four active coefficients and support under the same 1e-4*(1+z_i) tolerance, and select the eligible candidate minimizing the inactive-preactivation squared discrepancy. Candidate seed is the low 63 bits of SHA256 of SMC23|family|puzzle_index|component|goal_index, independent of target labels and task outcomes. Abstain if none qualifies; report candidate count, residual mismatch and least-squares unconstrained floor. This is an intentionally strong physical input-matched control; the full-rank inactive encoder may make perfect matching impossible.

Run five native arms on only the selected goal token: clean, self-patch zero, feature kill h-delta, matched sham h-sham and target mirror h+delta. All other 80 token states are unchanged at injection. Require exact equality of clean and self-patch full-vocabulary logits, and agreement to 1e-8 with independently computed clean source outputs. Fix the strongest non-target clean digit as the single rival, and compute correct-digit margin m. Primary per-goal contrast D=[m(clean)-m(kill)]-[m(clean)-m(sham)]=m(sham)-m(kill). Record both correct-digit flip rates and raw signed margins; all feature choices and sham rankings precede reading target digits.

## Frozen primary statistical gate

All admitted target-native top-one goals enter the primary analysis, **regardless of whether their feature appears on the calibration-beneficial list** and regardless of whether the teacher predicted the goal digit correctly. Report every pre-outcome eligible/ineligible goal, the token-weighted mean D and the per-puzzle mean of each puzzle's admitted goal contrasts; missing-puzzle count remains explicit.

Resample 64 puzzle IDs with replacement in 2,000 fixed-seed bootstrap draws, preserving all eight goal interventions within a puzzle. Each B teacher/SAE/cohort cell (4×2=8) is informative only if at least 60 eligible goals from at least 30 distinct puzzles and at least 1,990 valid bootstrap draws exist. Report descriptive 95% bounds and the more stringent **Bonferroni 99.375% interval across eight B comparisons**. The global seed root is 20262317 combined with a stable SHA256 of family and component.

**Registered positive teacher/SAE/cohort gate:** coverage passes; 99.375% puzzle-bootstrap lower bound on mean D > +0.02 digit-margin logits; and strictly more clean-correct-to-wrong answer flips under kill than sham. A pair passes only on BOTH new 30-hole and 34-hole families. A program-level success needs at least one SAE from EACH of B501/B701 to pass both families. Preserve nonpositive, null, and underpowered outcomes. The A reference has no familywise decision role.

For the frozen calibration-beneficial top-eight lists, report a separate descriptive subset on test goals with at least 24 eligible goals in 20 puzzles, with 95% puzzle-bootstrap intervals only; these cannot replace the primary gate. Further diagnostics: absolute output effect, clean-correct-only margins, mirror-arm effects, intervention dose and decoder-contribution ratio, sham/kill feature fidelity, inactive-preactivation match, full inactive-encoder rank and eligibility counts. Model SAE seeds are correlated within each teacher; puzzles, not tokens, are the independent resampling units.

## Interpretation

A positive gate establishes functional influence of an *endogenously active* target SAE coordinate under a matched-norm inactive-preactivation-matched sham on the specified three-step teacher-native Sudoku margin, conditional on the frozen affine encoder cell. Changes to previously inactive coordinates and other hidden representations can still explain residual effects; an intrinsic semantic feature identity or global quotient remains unproven. Neither a clean digit margin nor one goal's prediction flip establishes full-grid Sudoku skill improvement. Keep all older SMC-16/17 receipts and failures unchanged and leave the ARG-03-N 168 sealed test cases untouched. A standalone standard-library auditor must reconstruct all denominators, objective contrasts and pass/fail decisions from original raw receipts.
