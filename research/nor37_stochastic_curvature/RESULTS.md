## Question and experimental change

NOR-35 demonstrated a large high-curvature attack detection gain from moving half of an original NOR25 auditor's per-atom inspection quota to a PUBLIC deterministic highest-curvature prefix. NOR-36's informed precommitted greedy attackers exploited that prefix, reducing curvature50 hybrid50 self-play detection to 1.26% on its prior cohort. NOR-37 keeps the original per-source/trial **eight tail/top-magnitude/lag atom inspection quotas exactly fixed**, while randomizing targeted positions inside a high-curvature window and requiring positive whole-atom uniform exploration probability.

There are **24 entirely new original RRC25 public-only inputs**, replicates 56–59 × observed degree 512/1024 × three original NOR21 wide families. The original native sixteen-template source/true-query acceptance verifier fixes full hypothetical query output before restoring the planted native generator. All 24 complete-public source digests are new and disjoint from **156 archived historical original sources** NOR26/30/31/32/33/34/35/36.

Every full auditor inspects exactly **k=16 authenticated true target-output integer coefficients**. Ten auditor distributions are graded at equal query budgets: original NOR25 hybrid50, per-atom-random hybrid50, deterministic curvature50 hybrid50, stochastic25/50/75 hybrid50, pure uniform16, original frozen NOR31 mixture, deterministic curvature-transformed mixture, and stochastic50 curvature-transformed mixture. Original NOR31 component weights remain [2,0,1,1,4,8]/16.

The new randomized treatment, on every source, original base policy, trial, and nonempty old-risk atom, samples a **full uniform t_j positions** with probability 25%, 50%, or 75% according to treatment. Otherwise it randomly selects h_j=ceil(t_j/2) positions from the highest-curvature public window of width min(n_j,max(2h_j,ceil(n_j/8))), then samples the remaining t_j−h_j uniformly from the rest of the atom. All 16 queries and all original eight atom quota values are preserved exactly. The primary treatment is **stochastic50**.

## Source-locked calibrated attack game

Each of the ten defender classes receives the identical calibration allowance: **256 prior policy schedules**, **8 four-position greedy attack restarts**, and a source-conditioned randomized tie ordering. The attacker searches ALL m=769 or 1537 future positions, repeatedly choosing the coordinate that minimizes how many CAL trial schedules detect any of its chosen changes. For mixture defenders the objective is the original fixed 16-quantum weighted sum of all component auditors' CAL exposure. Every attacker commits its four changed positions BEFORE sampling **512 distinct independently salted EVAL schedules**; neither the true target values nor the realized EVAL schedule are supplied to it.

Four separate source-public blind attack controls (each 8 source-conditioned sets) target TOP-curvature support, BOTTOM-curvature support, exact old-eight-atom quota-matched redraw and whole-output uniform positions. All four are fixed prior to EVAL. Registered PRIMARY science uses source-level paired changes in self-play and worst-class detection, and **exact Fraction conditional high-curvature attack detection** on public top-48 U and top-48 outside-U support. Each of the 24 source identities is the independent observational unit. Repeated greedy restarts, blind attack sets and audit schedules are paired observations within a source.

## Threat-model limits and next test

NOR37 compares restricted GREEDY precommitted attacks and synthetic RRC25 continuations. All audit seeds are PUBLIC SHA-derived reproducibility fixtures, not cryptographically hidden production coins. An authentic external true-target coefficient witness is an extra information channel assumed by this program. The positive exploration guarantee is conditional on the audited atom quota actually sampling a changed coordinate with positive probability. A deliberate attacker able to choose a zero-quota atom can still evade that auditor.

Future work can test changing the original eight atom quotas themselves, especially allocating reserve to source-public low-prior-risk atoms, while preserving the total k16 witness budget. The sensitivity results already show that greater within-atom exploration moves toward the empirically stronger pure-uniform policy, at a corresponding loss of curated high-curvature support detection.
