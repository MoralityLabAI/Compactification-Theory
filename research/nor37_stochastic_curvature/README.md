# NOR-37: randomized within-atom curvature with explicit uniform exploration

**Prospective registration:** [REGISTRATION.md](REGISTRATION.md), first commit a874019 on November 17, 2026, before NOR-37 source, attack, audit or outcome data.

NOR-36 found that the publicly known deterministic half-curvature prefix reduces policy-aware self-play detection even as the policy retains substantial gains on source-aligned high-curvature alterations. NOR-37 replaces the deterministic prefix with stochastic sampling from a publicly defined high-curvature window and adds an independent whole-atom uniform exploration branch.

The original NOR25 k=16 audit plan's exact eight old-risk atom inspection quota t_j is preserved for every source, component arm and challenge. In each nonempty atom the stochastic25, stochastic50 and stochastic75 variants enter a full-uniform within-atom query branch with respective probabilities 25%, 50% and 75%. Otherwise, they sample ceil(t_j/2) random coordinates from the top public curvature window of size min(n_j,max(2ceil(t_j/2),ceil(n_j/8))), then fill the remaining slots uniformly from the atom without replacement. The original NOR35 deterministic curvature50 and atom-random controls remain unchanged. Every auditor reads exactly 16 authentic target-output integer coefficients.

Ten auditor distributions, including the frozen original NOR31 mixture, deterministic transformed mixture, stochastic50 transformed mixture and pure uniform16, receive symmetric policy-aware attacks trained with eight four-coordinate greedy restarts on 256 CAL challenge schedules. Attacks are selected over the entire m=769/1537 output-coefficient space and precommitted BEFORE 512 separately salted held-out challenges. Four extra source-public fixed blind attack families include top curvature, bottom curvature, full eight-atom quota matched and whole-output uniform positions.

Twenty-four new complete-public source-distinct RRC25 TEST problems from replicates 56–59, observed degree 512/1024 and three original families must be disjoint from 156 archived historical source identities. The public source-only fixture is frozen before attack generation or private original program reconstruction.

Five registered gates test whether stochastic50 hybrid50 improves against its own greedy attacker and across the WORST of ten calibrated attacker classes relative to deterministic curvature50, retains a measurable exact conditional TOP-curvature support advantage, improves robustness in the frozen six-arm NOR31 mixture, and preserves original native-program/witness/atom-count/independent full replay integrity. **Scientific FAIL is retained** on any missed gate, with byte-for-byte original source/grade evidence. Attacks and challenge rolls are paired within each of 24 independent source identities.

[MathCan NOR37](https://github.com/patrickdugan/MathCan/pull/132) formalizes the conditional exploration branch coverage floor for a BLIND attack fixed before independent audit coins, inherited exact NOR25 reserve counts after public calibration, and quota-matched detection invariance for uniform-within-atom attack laws. Positive exploration mass gives a nonzero detection contribution only when some attacked atom is actually inspected with positive probability. A full-schedule-informed adversary still has unchecked target-coordinate evasion. Authentic external target-output witness values and deployed private entropy are assumed; publicly replayable SHA streams do not constitute cryptographic secrecy.

**First prospective registered result: PASS on all five gates.** The first four-stage [Actions #44079786124](https://github.com/MoralityLabAI/Compactification-Theory/actions/runs/44079786124) verified 24 new source-distinct RRC25 inputs, 256 CAL/512 independent EVAL audit separation and a source-locked independent reconstruction of **1,920 greedy attacks**, **768 blind controls**, **663,552 constituent audit plans** and **30,277,632 decisions**. Stochastic50 hybrid50 improved its own calibrated-attack detection **+1.007 pp** and sourcewise worst of ten calibrated attacker classes **+1.046 pp** versus deterministic NOR35 curvature50, with **24/24 source wins** on both. High-curvature exact conditional detection beat atom random by **+5.687 pp**, retaining **32.65%** of deterministic curvature advantage. The frozen six-arm stochastic50 mixture improved its own calibrated-attack detection **+1.312 pp** and high-curvature detection **+5.933 pp** versus the registered controls, again on 24/24 sources. The 25% and 75% exploration policies quantify the robustness versus targeted-coverage tradeoff. Pure uniform16 remains strongest on measured worst calibrated class, 5.269% detection.
