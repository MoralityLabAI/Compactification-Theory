## Scientific problem and intervention

NOR-33 found an average 3.116 percentage-point curvature-versus-matched-random **hybrid_50 advantage** at exactly two of four changed coefficients in the old historical tail∪magnitude∪lag risk union. Matching that coarse union leaves seven membership subpatterns unspecified. NOR-34 isolates these eight joint patterns (tail bit 1, top-magnitude bit 2, lag bit 4), allowing a source-public risk score to distinguish cells that share the same coarse union membership.

Twenty-four new RRC25 complete-public-input sources use replicates 44–47, observed demonstration degrees 512/1024 and the same three original wide input families, with complete-public SHA identities disjoint from **84** prior NOR26/30/31/32/33 source cases. The exact original sixteen-template public verifier freezes its candidate future from only observed demonstrations and public query INPUT before restoring the planted original program and true query OUTPUT.

The full proposed source-visible curvature ranking supplies a fixed support of **48 positions within** the historical risk union and **48 positions outside**. Each source has sixteen matched attack triplets:

- Curvature: 2 randomly changed coordinates in curated curvature U support and 2 in curated curvature V support.
- Coarse: 2 random changes anywhere in old U and 2 anywhere in its complement V.
- Eight-atom: preserve the curvature attack's **exact** joint eight-category change-count vector, then redraw each category quota independently from that full public category.

All attack placements precede the frozen 512 audit schedules. Every pure policy reads k=16 exact external target-output coefficient values. The mixture uses unchanged NOR31 integer weights [2,0,1,1,4,8]/16 over uniform16, static, posterior, and hybrid_25/50/75. Both nonzero changes +1 and +4,294,049,777 produce identical exact-witness detections.

## Exact conditional law and verification

For old risk atom j with n_j coordinates and t_j checked positions, an attack that changes exactly q_j positions in that atom has **C(n_j−t_j,q_j) missed configurations** out of C(n_j,q_j) total configurations. The full eight-atom law has the product of these counts across all atoms, after conditioning on the attack's public atom quota vector q.

The primary code computes exact Fraction expectations for the random curvature-support two-plus-two draw and its subsequent category-quota redraw, using unordered pairs of occupied atom labels and public category population counts. These fractions are averaged over every original audit schedule, without relying on a finite sixteen-attack Monte Carlo estimate. [MathCan NOR-34 PR #126](https://github.com/patrickdugan/MathCan/pull/126) proves the exact all-atom product-count theorem, conditional sufficiency of matched atom inspection counts, and a finite coarse-union counterexample in Lean 4.35.

The source-level result strongly supports a particular mechanistic explanation: the **three old support-membership indicator bits capture essentially all of the curvature-specific hybrid-versus-mixture detection advantage on this synthetic source roster and auditor family**. This fits the policy architecture, which ranks coordinates largely by those fixed old membership signatures. Within-atom coordinate selection still creates small individual source residuals.
