# NOR-34: eight old-risk membership atoms explain curvature residual?

**Original prospective protocol:** [REGISTRATION.md](REGISTRATION.md), first commit ef6d7b4, made before any NOR-34 source, attack or result.

NOR-33 found a curvature-specific hybrid_50 detection advantage even after matching how many of four altered output positions intersect the union of historical tail, top-magnitude and lag risk regions. NOR-34 conditions on their **entire three-bit joint membership**, separating eight public risk atoms.

The frozen original NOR-31 mixture [2,0,1,1,4,8]/16 and its six constituent k=16 exact coefficient auditors are unchanged. The source generator draws **24 new RRC25 public-only examples**, disjoint from 84 original historical source IDs. A source-public curvature ranking fixes 48 supported positions inside the historical union and 48 outside. Sixteen four-coordinate curvature attacks draw two positions from each block. For every original attack, an eight-atom matched attack preserves its precise bit-pattern quota while sampling from the full corresponding atom; a coarse matched attack preserves only the 2+2 union split. All attacks freeze before 512 independent audit schedules per source and arm. Exact native target reconstruction occurs only after these public and attack decisions.

The primary statistical quantities are **analytical, exact conditional-attack detection expectations**, computed in Fraction arithmetic over frozen source/audit schedules and averaged across each of 24 source identities. Finite sampled attacks are separately measured for calibration. The full eight-atom matched law integrates the category quota distribution induced by selecting two curvature-support positions in each of the historical union blocks, and redraws each quota uniformly within the complete corresponding public atom. The registered five gates test whether matching eight membership bits attenuates ≥70% of NOR-33's coarse curvature residual, leaves ≤0.5 pp mean remainder, and agrees with finite matched sampled-attack comparisons.

The companion [MathCan NOR-34](https://github.com/patrickdugan/MathCan/pull/126) proves exact multi-atom fixed-quota attack counts and the equality of audit policies with identical per-atom inspection counts. Coarse old-union counts may match while per-atom conditional risk differs.

**First prospective registered result: PASS on all five gates.** The [original four-stage green GitHub Actions run #44054626746](https://github.com/MoralityLabAI/Compactification-Theory/actions/runs/44054626746) completed 24 new source cases and a separate-process independent replayer covering **3,538,944 audit/world decisions** and **27,648 direct exact witness checks**. The exact conditional curvature-minus-coarse residual was **+2.6741 percentage points**, positive on 24/24 sources. Matching all eight tail/magnitude/lag memberships reduced the source-mean residual to **+0.003205 pp**, with 24/24 per-source absolute residual reductions. That is **99.880% attenuation of the signed source-mean residual** and **96.256% attenuation in mean absolute source-level residual**.
