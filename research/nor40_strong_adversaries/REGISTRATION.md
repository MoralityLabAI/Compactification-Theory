## Negative controls and limits

A fully realized-schedule-informed adversary can change four unchecked positions and evade. Public-only curvature weights can leak predictable targeted prefixes. The attacker-calibration experiment is finite computational evidence about a specified attacker budget rather than a proof of globally optimal support choices. Do not conflate empirical 512-EVAL hit frequencies with that ideal probability floor, or an exact restricted-pool optimum with an unrestricted minimax certificate.
