# NOR-40: stronger policy-aware auditors' adversaries

The first NOR-40 prospective [REGISTRATION.md](REGISTRATION.md) was frozen in commit `dbc1adbe0e30b9c0e36421cf07cebcf376d6a1ec` on November 17, 2026, before any NOR-40 source generation or data. [Experiment PR #225](https://github.com/MoralityLabAI/Compactification-Theory/pull/225) develops [design issue #224](https://github.com/MoralityLabAI/Compactification-Theory/issues/224).

**Fixed question:** With a policy-aware attacker allowed a larger 2048-schedule CAL budget, 32 full-output greedy starts, local swaps and an exact search over 1,820 four-element subsets of a CAL-selected 16-coordinate pool, does the preregistered `global7_mix` auditor retain an advantage over `deterministic_mix` while remaining competitive with `original_mix` and preserving source-aligned public curvature detection? Every defender pays 16 authentic target-output coefficient comparisons and faces an attack changing four source-output positions fixed before 512 separate hidden EVAL schedules.

The predeclared **CAL256/8 greedy baseline** is trained on the first 256 of the same 2048 CAL schedules. All baseline and strong attacks are then graded against the *same* independently salted heldout schedules. This isolates what the extra CAL and stronger optimization buy without adjusting the defender or audit budget. The stronger search tests 32 seeded greedy restarts, eight selected single-swap refinements and an exact C(16,4)=1820 candidate-pool enumeration; its eight selected attacks are chosen only from CAL. Source-level objective brackets report **L: fourth-smallest singleton union** and **U: best tested radius-four support**, so the true global CAL minimum lies within [L,U]. The exact subset optimum is explicitly restricted to the nominated 16 candidates, with no global solver certificate implied.

The 48 new public-only original RRC25 sources are registered as replicates 76–83 × observed degrees 512 and 1024 × three original NOR21 families. They exclude all 276 previous original NOR26–39 full public-source SHA identities, with NOR39's original first fixture/grade SHA pinned. Tests use archived NOR39 sources or synthetic exposure masks, and a separate first-fixture job freezes the entire new public-only roster before private target reconstruction, CAL attack construction or EVAL grading. Original source/grade bytes and scientific PASS or FAIL remain unchanged after first scoring.

Seven attacker classes each have **eight baseline and eight strong attacks**. The six separately frozen controls include top and bottom curvature, eight-atom quota-matched, whole-output uniform, low CAL exposure and zero-risk-union stress. The complete 20 attacker × 7 defender crossplay, CAL certificate receipts, per-source primary contrasts and eight replicate-block sensitivity are recorded for independent replay. Unrestricted uniform16 is the analytic full-output minimax defender under hidden independent audit coins; the `global7_mix` branch retains the conditional (7/16) times uniform coverage floor. Public SHA research streams are deterministic reproducibility fixtures and the true target-output witness is an external assumption.
