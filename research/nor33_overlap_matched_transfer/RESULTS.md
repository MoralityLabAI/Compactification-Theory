## Identification: old-risk overlap and within-union placement

NOR-32's nominally unfamiliar curvature96 mechanism had selected substantial coordinates from the old magnitude and tail risk sectors. NOR-33 intervenes on *actual* support overlap with the historical union U = tail96 ∪ magnitude96 ∪ lag96, calculated from the public predicted query continuation.

Each of **24 fresh source-distinct RRC25 inputs** (replicates 40–43, observation degrees 512/1024 and three NOR-21 wide families) supplies seven fixed complete **public-only ranking mechanisms**: central, stride17, low_abs, curvature, anti_prior, source hash and stratified_random. Each mechanism produces **five matched 96-position attack supports** with exactly 0/24/48/72/96 positions inside U, and eight radius-four attacks from each support with exactly 0/1/2/3/4 altered coefficients inside U. This yields **840 source/support cells** and **6,720 fixed blind attack sets**. Every attack set was chosen before the 512 evaluation schedules per source and auditor were sampled.

Auditors use the unchanged NOR-25 k=16 exact coefficient checks: original pure uniform16, static ranking, posterior and hybrid_25/50/75. The frozen [NOR-31](https://github.com/MoralityLabAI/Compactification-Theory/pull/200) six-class mixture is [2,0,1,1,4,8]/16. The evaluator freezes full public 16-template predictions before reopening original private generating programs and exact native target outputs. All 24 source hashes are new relative to **60** historical NOR-26/30/31/32 complete-public source identities. Every arm uses **exactly 16 authenticated integer target coefficient queries** per trial. The exact external target witness is an *additional assumed information channel*.

## Analytical conditional expectation recorded before grading

The registration fixed the per-plan exact probability for a support of 96 positions containing a=24z coordinates in U and b=96−a outside U, with a realized auditor inspecting t_U and t_V positions in those *specific support blocks*. A uniformly sampled conditional attack changing z in the first part and 4−z in the second is detected with probability

\[
1-\frac{\binom{a-t_U}{z}\binom{b-t_V}{4-z}}
        {\binom{a}{z}\binom{b}{4-z}}.
\]

This formula is kernel-proved in [MathCan NOR-33 #125](https://github.com/patrickdugan/MathCan/pull/125). The experiment recorded its mean across all 512 fixed auditor plans for every source, family and z alongside sampled eight-attack results. **Retrospective diagnostic using those preregistered exact expectation fields**, without generating new attacks, gives:

| z | Sampled-attack mean d(z), pp | Exact conditional-attack mean d(z), pp |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 0 | +0.8877 | +0.8396 |
| 1 | +0.0341 | +0.0904 |
| 2 | −0.7697 | −0.6889 |
| 3 | −1.2130 | −1.3058 |
| 4 | −2.1516 | −2.0221 |

The analytically integrated endpoint gradient is **+2.8617 pp**, positive on 24/24 sources. The curvature-vs-random residual at z=2 is **+3.0082 pp**, positive on 23/24. These are secondary re-aggregations of **already registered per-plan exact expectations**, rather than changes to the original primary PASS/FAIL conditions. They limit the interpretation that the observed monotone gradient was produced by eight sampled attack draws alone.

## Limits of the result

This is a controlled intervention on the **support selection and attack placement distribution**, not an unconstrained attacker's response. The exact-matched attack distribution is constructed deliberately and differs from the original NOR-32 random-within-new-family protocol. New support rankings are public functions of a candidate future and source input; the source model stays within exact synthetic RRC25. The external target-coefficient witness and independent, hidden audit challenge coins are assumptions. The seeded SHA-based Python simulations provide deterministic evidence replay, **not** deployed cryptographic unpredictability. An attacker who sees the realized complete partial audit schedule can choose unchecked changed coordinates and evade, just as prior NOR-23–32 results established.

The experimental independent unit is **24 source identities**. Multiple support families, attack restarts and 512 challenge trials per source are paired and correlated; all numerical results and source-win thresholds use those 24 source identities.
