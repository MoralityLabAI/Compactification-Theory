Retain the existing NOR44–49 program/session/provider signing boundary and existing negative controls.

## Exactly five frozen engineering gates
1. **Repeated inverse memoization:** require >=8 sources with signed active R count>=2 and median of their *sourcewise* `cached_direct / original_direct` ratios strictly **<0.70×**.
2. **Residual GMP advantage:** on the same >=8 multi-R sources, median of sourcewise `GMP_full_trace / cached_direct` strictly **<0.90×**. Failure establishes that the observed prior acceleration can largely be due to redundant native inverse work.
3. **Low-duplication overhead:** require >=8 sources with signed R count<=1 and median of their sourcewise `cached_direct / original_direct` ratios strictly **<1.10×**.
4. **Exact parity and signed controls:** 64/64 full exact output vectors and Merkle roots identical across all arms, each distinct active unary operation executes once in the new executor, and predata signed authority/session/provider/tamper controls pass.
5. **Original source, timing, and reentrancy controls:** exact 64 distinct source IDs disjoint from 452 historical IDs in registered 16 blocks, source-only upstream SHA freeze, five interleaved repeats, no NOR46 process-global convolution modification, exact signed integer bounds, and immutable first-run raw JSON/receipt SHA.

All five gates must pass for the first engineering PASS. If too few multi-R sources appear, the first two gates FAIL without resampling.
