## Formal and scientific interpretation

Under ideal independent sampling, the registered mixture retains exact source-independent lower detection bounds of **5.24841%** for m=769 output coefficients and **2.64670%** for m=1537, against any fixed blind four-position alteration set. These are mathematical conditional floors and should not be compared to a single observed per-source sample frequency as a strict empirical cutoff. The separate MathCan [NOR-31 class quotient theorem](https://github.com/patrickdugan/MathCan/pull/121) proves duplicate audit-class collapse and inherited conditional mixture coverage.

**Pure uniform16 still has stronger observed policy-aware minimax detection**, averaging 5.98% against its own calibrated attacker and 5.41% in worst cross-play. The NOR-31 mixture is a purposeful **constrained coverage compromise**, retaining 17.36% in-template performance versus uniform's 6.05%, while improving on fixed hybrid_50 against the preregistered policy-aware attack classes. This is a finite empirical constrained Pareto point, not an unrestricted minimax optimum against arbitrary attackers.

A stronger attacker that learns the realized complete audit schedule can still select unchecked positions and evade. All claims assume an authentic external target-dependent exact witness and hidden independent audit coins. Public SHA-seeded challenge streams enable deterministic replay and provide no production cryptographic secrecy. The 8 attack restarts and 512 challenges within each of the 12 source identities are correlated and cannot be counted as independent source examples.
