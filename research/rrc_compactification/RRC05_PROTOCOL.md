# RRC-05: fixed CPU protocol and separation of evidence

**Scope.** Exact finite-degree algebraic recovery and a two-scalar learned
correction over a publicly supplied divisor graph. No neural weights discover
the graph. No source equation, future coefficient or test generator enters
training. RRC-04's prior test roster is treated as exposed development
material. All RRC-05 test examples use fresh deterministic source seeds.

- **Observation:** integer coefficients of \(F\) at degrees 0–64. Exact
  integer preprocessing computes \(b_n\) via \(qF'/F\) before the new
  learned models receive \(c_n=b_n/n\). This is counted as external symbolic
  computation. The frozen historical RRC-04 students see raw coefficients.
- **Training:** 384 unique-prefix random periodic-product sources with
  multiplicity cap unlimited, one or two; moduli 2,3,4,6,8,9,10,12.
  Generated with seed 2026110905+101. Development is 96 independent
  in-modulus sources, seed +202; training and development prefixes must
  be disjoint. Supervisory targets are independent exact Euler inversion.
- **Unseen-modulus test:** 20 independent sources for each held-out
  modulus 5,7,11,13,17,19 (120 total), generated by seed +303.
- **Held-out source-family test:** 40 local gap grammars (gaps 1–5,
  smallest part 1–8); 15 nonperiodic part sets across pentagonal,
  centered-hexagonal, cubes, semiprimes, and primes 1 mod 4 (caps 0,1,2);
  and 18 outside-the-declared-grammar periodic sets with periods 23,29,31.
- **Observational collisions:** six base/mutant pairs from period-13
  products with an added factor at degrees 65,77,89,105,116,127. Both
  members share their complete prefix. Acceptance cannot certify either
  future; record false acceptances and the one-in-two ceiling.
- **Grammar:** up to period 20 with a two-occurrences-per-residue gate,
  caps unlimited/1/2. Syntax bits are two cap bits, Elias-gamma period,
  then period mask. The full decoder, oracle, graph, model weights and
  coefficient vectors are separate costs.
- **Learned architecture:** shared two-parameter residual gains
  \(\alpha,\beta\) trained with 300 Adam steps, batch 96, lr 0.06,
  seven passes, a combined exponent-MSE and divisor-residual loss
  (weights 1 and 0.2). A separately trained two-parameter one-pass
  feedforward comparator sees the identical preprocessed input. A
  shuffled-target recurrent control uses the same architecture and
  optimizer without the true residual objective.
- **Depth experiment:** freeze the trained gains, evaluate 0,1,2,4,6,7,8
  iterations. Other controls are a parameter-free exact Jacobi recurrence,
  the frozen RRC-04 raw student checkpoints (only periods <=12), and
  removal of the proper-divisor coupling at seven iterations.
- **Evaluation:** quantize proposed exponents; record first exact
  divisor/series residual, candidate executable grammar, observed-prefix
  equality, and accuracy across all withheld degrees 65–128. Use the
  exact bounded-period symbolic solver as a ceiling within its grammar.
  Separate held-out family metrics and count incidental prefix aliases.
- **Audit:** standard-library deterministic replay checks all sources,
  checkpoint SHA-256s, per-case hashes and frozen JSON receipt. The
  training command is optional and requires CPU PyTorch; CI only
  runs local standard-library tests and frozen replay. No cloud/GPU/model
  downloads or stochastic network-dependent work are involved.

**Interpretation gate.** A learned recurrence success is credited only as
fidelity to the prewired inversion circuit. End-to-end discovery from raw
counts requires future direct-model evidence on an unexposed test roster.
The finite-prefix collision bound still governs full future correctness.
