## Why these audit depths

NOR31-M's retrospective original-root coverage diagnostic found, for the hybrid risk8+reserve8 auditor over 512 known schedules, a mean of 104.583 NEVER-inspected coefficients at m=1537, and 3.417 at m=769. Twelve of twelve m=1537 sources admitted four permanently uninspected coefficients. Under ideal independent uniform sampling, for N=m-8 unchecked-risk coordinates and n 8-probe reserve schedules, the expected number never inspected is N(1-8/N)^n. Crossing the threshold at which four completely untouched coordinates cease to exist can force a batch-fixed attacker who sees the entire batch to suffer some detection, even when it can minimize how often it is caught.

**Prospective question:** with the SAME realized root and NESTED sequence of evaluation schedules, does enlarging the leaked batch from 512 to 1024, 1536 and 2048 remove the existence of any radius-four attack that is untouched by ALL schedules? Separately, how do the empirical *per-schedule* detection rates of independent-root calibrated and root-aware greedy attacks change? Distinguish never-inspected batch feasibility, total hit counts and fractional hit rate.

## Exact attack information and fairness

Two attack conditions at each n, arm and source:
- **Independent calibration:** use the first n schedules under C, calculate each coordinate's n-schedule exposure mask and greedily choose a set of exactly r=4 changed coefficients minimizing the union of exposed schedule indices, with 32 fixed tie restarts. One set remains fixed across the n evaluation schedules. Commit each set with a source/arm/depth/restart-bound salted SHA-256 digest and fresh 32-byte OS nonce BEFORE E is sampled. All independent-root attacks over every n are frozen in PREP.
- **Leaked evaluation root:** after E has been sampled and its 2048-schedule sequence SHA-locked, the attacker gets all evaluation schedules, but for each registered n may train only on the first n. Apply the exact same greedy optimizer and **32 same tie seeds** to E's first n schedules. Commit these new attacks after E disclosure. Every attack remains fixed over that n-batch. This is an *in-sample optimization* by design, not a heldout efficacy claim.
- **Per-schedule informed** counterfactual: after seeing one realized schedule, choose four uninspected indices; it evades each challenge, but cannot open an earlier commitment under the SHA binding assumption. This is stronger than one batch-fixed attack.

All 24 × 3 × 4 × 32 = **9216 independent-root precommit attacks**, and the same **9216 post-root compromised attacks** have separately salted 256-bit nonces and commitments. Verify every opening and both a nonce change and changed-position mutation. For each source, arm, depth n and condition, grade all 32 attacks on SAME evaluation first-n schedule batch. Exactly 24×3×2×32×(512+1024+1536+2048)=**23,592,960 distinct fixed-batch decisions**, plus 24×3×2048 = **147,456** per-schedule informed controls. The two exact nonzero deltas +1 and +4294049777 are checks on each same decision, not independent trials.

## Primary gates (all frozen before new data)

**Registered PASS iff:**
1. For **m=1537 hybrid_50**, at depth 512, at least **10/12** sources have at least r=4 NEVER-inspected positions in their complete first-512 evaluation batches, while at depth 2048 at most **2/12** do. The number of sources permitting a fully missed batch-fixed attack must decrease by at least **8/12**. This is a coverage-feasibility endpoint, grounded in the original NOR31-M 12/12 at 512 and the ideal coupon-collector expectation (~104 at n=512, ~0.03 at n=2048).
2. The independent-calibration minus leaked-root **hybrid_50 per-schedule detection** at depth **2048** is positive on at least **20/24 source pairs** with equal-source mean difference at least **+0.005 absolute**. This tests that enough audit coverage to remove all-uninspected positions still leaves a root-informed selection advantage, and is distinct from the possibility of zero total hits.
3. Across every source and auditor, untouched-coordinate counts are **nonincreasing** across nested depths, and four-coordinate batch-zero-hit feasibility (count>=4) cannot reappear once lost. All 9216 original commitments open before OS E root creation, no nonce/coordinate mutation or changed late opening reopens earlier commitments, both independent OS roots are distinct, and PREP artifacts are byte-locked before E is minted.
4. Independent separate-process replay reproduces both 2048-schedule rooted sequences, source-native masks/programs and the **23,592,960** exact fixed-batch decisions and source-level gates without importing NOR32 source, optimizer, sampler, grader or evaluator. Both nonzero coefficient shifts agree; legitimate unchanged targets pass authentic exact witness.
5. All **147,456** informed per-schedule attacks evade completely, and their newly constructed changed supports cannot reopen any earlier salted digest (under the SHA-256 binding assumption). The original independent-calibration attack decisions are unchanged under later root disclosure by protocol.

If any scientific gate fails, retain the observed registered FAIL and all first original receipts without changing thresholds. The independent unit is the new source (24). The four nested depths, 32 attack restarts, phase roots and 2048 schedule ordinals are correlated within each source. Report per-source paired effects and counts, empirical source proportions for feasibility, source-block confidence as *descriptive*, coverage by m and policy, the ratio of each attack's total hit count to n, first expected n thresholds and actual minimum/median number of hits. Do not assert that **fractional** detection must rise monotonically as the depth increases: the optimized attack changes with n and the denominator n also changes.
