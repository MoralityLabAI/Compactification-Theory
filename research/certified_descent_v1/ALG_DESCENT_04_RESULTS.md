New priority theorem: fixed independent candidate successes and continuation values give optimal retry order C_i/p_i+V_i by adjacent exchange. For schoolbook work, subtract invariant quadratic potential to get structural overhead plus C_i(1/p_i-1). Correlated proposals require conditional risk, so this is only a heuristic in the gym. Exact relaxed structural bound L(d) uses ordered positive degree partitions and all allowed arities. MathCan companion gives proofs; independent audit enumerates 4,459 compositions and 11,594 permutations.
