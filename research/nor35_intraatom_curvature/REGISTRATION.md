## Formal mathematical companion

Let each atom have size n_j, base source-specific inspection quota t_j, deterministic targeted prefix c_j<=t_j, and uniform reserve u_j=t_j−c_j. If an attack of r_j changed positions in that atom intersects the targeted prefix, the exact missed reserve count is zero. If it avoids the prefix, the exact number of reserve draws of size u_j missing every changed coordinate is C(n_j−c_j−r_j,u_j), out of C(n_j−c_j,u_j). The product across atoms gives the exact conditional miss count under independent per-atom reserve draws. Match all base t_j and quota-conditioned attack distributions: original and transformed policies have identical conditional expected detection for every fixed q_j vector under uniform redraw within each full atom (NOR34). A fully schedule-informed attacker remains able to change unchecked positions.

The proof's premises include independent genuine random reserve draws, attack fixation before actual audit coins, and a trustworthy extra target-dependent coefficient witness. Public SHA replay streams do not establish cryptographic secrecy.
