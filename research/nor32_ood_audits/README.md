# NOR-32: transfer to six procedurally unseen alteration supports

**Prospective registration:** [REGISTRATION.md](REGISTRATION.md), the first NOR-32 commit (07f0f91211c21b3a7ed540ebcb246faffc7377e0) made before any new NOR-32 source or result. Continues [NOR-31 merged PR #200](https://github.com/MoralityLabAI/Compactification-Theory/pull/200).

NOR-31 improved source-held-out calibrated attacker coverage with a six-class nonalias auditor mixture, at a 16-coordinate exact-witness budget, while imposing a template-detection floor on the training game. NOR-32 asks whether those weights transfer to six **new support-generation mechanisms** absent from NOR-26 and NOR-30 historical training: central96, stride17_96, low_abs96, curvature96, anti_prior96 and hash96. Each mechanism selects 96 candidate changed positions from the complete publicly proposed output. The new support sets may overlap historical regions; the family-generating rules are independent of training.

Twenty-four fresh public-only RRC25 source inputs at replicates 32–35, degrees 512/1024 and three old source families are generated in a separate, dependent CI job. Full public source digests are disjoint from the 36 pinned historical NOR-26/30/31 identities. All private original generating programs and exact hidden target outputs remain sealed until audit policies and prospective attack/challenge sets are fixed. Native acceptance masks and hypothetical query-output unanimity are checked with the unchanged original 16-template exact interpreter.

At each source/family, 8 independently chosen fixed-blind four-coordinate attacks are PRIMARY. Four further greedy attack sets are calibrated against each of the mixture, pure uniform and hybrid_50 using 256 **calibration** challenge schedules; they are separately scored against 512 **held-out** challenge schedules. Every arm reads exactly 16 target-output coefficients. Original tail/magnitude/lag/uniform template controls and a fully schedule-informed evasive adversary establish transfer and assumption boundaries. The frozen NOR-31 mixture is [2,0,1,1,4,8]/16 across uniform, static, posterior and hybrid_25/50/75.

The registration freezes five gates: blind OOD mixture superiority over hybrid_50 in at least 18/24 source pairs with +0.2 percentage point mean gain, worst-OOD-family superiority on at least 16/24, at least 60% of pure uniform OOD blind coverage, policy-aware OOD gains on at least 16/24 with nonnegative mean and at least 15% old-template retention, plus original source/witness and independent replay controls. Every source, support, attack, audit and class-level count is preserved regardless of scientific PASS/FAIL.

**First registered outcome: SCIENTIFIC FAIL.** The [original evaluation](https://github.com/MoralityLabAI/Compactification-Theory/actions/runs/44042332472) completed all 24 new source cases, and a corrected independent replayer in [Actions #44042944819](https://github.com/MoralityLabAI/Compactification-Theory/actions/runs/44042944819) reconstructed all 144 distinct OOD support definitions, 3,648 attacks, **11,206,656 audit decisions**, and **87,552 direct exact witness checks** under both nonzero integer shifts. The original source fixture and outcome were committed unchanged in [results/nor32_original_hosted.zip](results/nor32_original_hosted.zip), with [frozen first-run SHA receipt](results/nor32_first_frozen_receipt.json), and permanent read-only CI validates those exact bytes before re-evaluating every count.

The mixture won the preregistered primary OOD-blind comparison on only **4/24** fresh source pairs versus hybrid_50, with mean **−0.5283 percentage points** (the frozen requirements were ≥18/24 and ≥+0.2 points). Four other registered gates passed, including 24/24 worst-family wins and 19/24 calibrated OOD self-play wins. The curvature96 attack generator overlaps old high-magnitude risk supports on 74.75/96 coordinates on average and strongly favors hybrid_50: **26.17%** detection versus mixture **20.20%**. The original result remains FAIL. See [RESULTS.md](RESULTS.md) for the complete six-family table, source-level interpretations and trust assumptions.

The abstract mathematical companion will prove the finite **uniform-attack duality**: every fixed size-k audit schedule has the same expected detection under an independent uniform r-position alteration, whereas concentration on structured supports necessarily changes relative coverage across nonuniform alteration families. The application is synthetic exact RRC25 and assumes a trustworthy external target-coefficient witness. Public SHA challenge seeds enable replay but cannot provide deployment secrecy.
