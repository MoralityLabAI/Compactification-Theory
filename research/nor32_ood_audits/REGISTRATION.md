## Six previously untrained size-96 OOD support mechanisms

All are fully specified by public prediction y of length m, public header, and public source digest h:
1. **central96:** integer indices from floor(m/2)-48 through floor(m/2)+47.
2. **stride17_96:** indices (int(h[:16],16)+17*j) mod m for j=0,...,95. Both m choices are coprime to 17 and the set has 96 distinct positions.
3. **low_abs96:** the 96 smallest abs(y[i]), breaking ties by smaller index.
4. **curvature96:** the 96 largest cyclic abs(y[(i-1) mod m]-2*y[i]+y[(i+1) mod m]), ties by smaller index.
5. **anti_prior96:** 96 smallest frozen NOR-24 per-index risk scores from original tail/magnitude/lag/uniform support mixture, ties by smaller index.
6. **hash96:** 96 smallest SHA256 digests of UTF-8 "NOR32|"+h+"|"+decimal(i), ties by smaller index, a public source-bound random-like control.

These mechanisms have no fitted model parameters. Log intersection counts between each new and each historic structured template. Exactly 96 unique positions in every support is a mandatory integrity check.

## Formal MathCan companion

For any finite support of s possible changed output positions, a fixed audit inspecting t support positions detects a radius-r uniformly drawn alteration from that support with exact probability 1−C(s−t,r)/C(s,r). For uniformly sampled fixed-radius attacks from the entire m-coordinate universe, every possible size-k inspection set has EXACTLY the same mean detection 1−C(m−k,r)/C(m,r), independent of how its positions were chosen. Hence randomized mixtures of k-coordinate audits share that uniform-attack average; improving one particular source-supported attack distribution may decrease coverage elsewhere. Prove these exact combinatorial counts using NOR-23's standalone finite subset enumerator and Boolean partition. Include the existing constructive schedule-informed evasive alternative-future theorem as a conditional counterexample.

The mathematics assumes an honest exogenous exact target witness and a truly independent sampled challenge unknown to attackers who choose attacks first. It does not establish SHA/HMAC cryptographic security, OS entropy quality or soundness of arbitrary theorem provers.
