## Cohorts and model

Three frozen synthetic q=3, b=2 broadcast cells with
lambda=4/5. Every tree is independently generated by
the exact channel mixture (copy parent with probability
4/5, otherwise draw a fresh uniform state). Only the
counts of retained terminal labels enter the classifier.
Roots are uniform and supplied as supervised labels
on training trees only.

Each cell has 4096 training and 4096 independently
generated heldout whole trees. All classifiers use
the same per-cell train/test splits and a frozen feature
vector with three normalized category counts, retained
fraction, and intercept.

The newly trained multiclass logistic model has
15 float64 learned coefficients, trained with six
online-SGD epochs at learning rate 0.15.
Supervised root-label fits at seeds 11 and 19 have
matched random-uniform training-label control fits.
All static model coefficients are charged separately
from the live q-counter state.

## Independent heldout results

| Observation condition | Exact count MAP (population) | Heldout plurality | Supervised seed 11 | Supervised seed 19 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Depth 3, all 8 leaves | 82.35% | 83.37% | 81.67% | 80.03% |
| Depth 2, 50% retention | 75.63% | 74.76% | 75.90% | 74.32% |
| Depth 2, sparse retention 625/1024 | 78.68% | 78.17% | 78.34% | 77.71% |

The exact count MAP column is a population probability
computed by exhaustively summing all possible observed
leaf patterns. The other columns are sample accuracies
on 4096 heldout trees. A heldout estimate above the
exact population optimum is possible through finite
sampling and does not violate Bayes optimality.

The supervised model learned a useful signal in every
cell. It does not improve on the fixed plurality
baseline across the complete-depth-three sample.
This experiment therefore demonstrates efficient
synthetic learning and the competitive strength of
a fixed count decoder, rather than superior model
performance.

## Matched random-label controls

| Observation condition | Random-label seed 11 | Random-label seed 19 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depth 3, all leaves | 16.09% | 8.11% |
| Depth 2, 50% retention | 33.30% | 15.97% |
| Depth 2, sparse retention | 24.56% | 52.37% |

The control deliberately fits uniform random source
labels using identical optimizer steps, architecture,
and input data. It may obtain very low or
chance-aligned apparent accuracy under different
training seeds because its learned class preferences
are unrelated to the true root. No performance gate
selects the better seed; all arms are retained.

## Resource and proof boundaries

At depth three with complete data, the count
monitor requires at most 8 mutable counter bits,
compared with 8 observed trinary leaf symbols.
The 15 learned float64 coefficients occupy
at least 960 bits in a flat double-precision
model representation, with additional executable
code and numeric overhead. These are distinct
resource categories. With partial observation at
depth two, all three counts can be stored
in at most 9 mutable bits.

No trained language model, Q5 LoopedTransformers
checkpoint, or infinite-tree factor-of-IID
sampling algorithm was evaluated. The finite
synthetic result does not establish the
non-reconstruction direction of source #229
or a coding-radius guarantee for source #236.
