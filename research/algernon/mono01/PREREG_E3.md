# MONO-01 E3 pre-registration (written 2026-11-04 before any E3 outcome exists)

Question: can covariance geometry plus causal localization produce, before the first edited generation, a rank-one `mlp.down_proj` edit that removes a model's looping behavior as well as or better than the published Qwen3.5-0.8B p50c form, and does the predicted preservation cost order the damage?

Substrates: Qwen3.5-0.8B bf16 (Qwen/Qwen3.5-0.8B at 2fc06364715b967f1860aea9cf38778875588b17) and Qwen3.5-9B NF4 (`techwithsergiu/Qwen3.5-text-9B-bnb-4bit` at 220c703421073067939e7ad5cd733b5a85e07118, a third-party bitsandbytes NF4 quantization; the substrate is that 4-bit model, not the official bf16 checkpoint).

Fixed before running (`phase_e3.py`): GSM8K practice problems (exp8_math/practice, none from the evaluation sets), permutation seed 20261104; disjoint calibration, teacher-forced held-out and test rosters (hash recorded in `meta.json`); thinking on; sampling temperature 0.6, top-p 0.95, top-k 20; loop tokens = tokens inside 8-grams already seen twice in the same generation; edit support 64 input columns; published coefficient c = 6; edits applied as factorized fp32 hooks (never merged to bf16).

Procedure: (1) generate on the calibration roster; (2) one batched teacher-forced pass collects per-layer down_proj input reservoirs and loop/non-loop output means, giving a loop direction u per layer; (3) the proxy score of a layer is the drop in loop-token log-probability minus the drop in non-loop log-probability when u is projected out, on calibration texts; the two highest-scoring layers are edited; (4) arms: A_pub_c6 (columns with the largest |u^T W|, W' = W - 6 u (mask * u^T W)), R_dir_c6 (random direction), B_greedy/B_beam/B_rand (minimum-energy rank-one edit under the non-loop activation covariance on a 64-column support chosen by conditional-leverage greedy, beam width 4, or at random from a 256-column key pool), B_full_c6 (dense minimum-energy via Woodbury), B_greedy_c1, and for the 0.8B the rebuilt published p50c tensors; (5) predicted cost tr(D C D^T) and held-out teacher-forced damage are computed for every arm before any test-roster generation.

Primary outcome: mean loop-token fraction per generation on the frozen test roster, paired difference against the unedited model with a paired bootstrap 95% interval. Secondary: generations cut at the token limit, GSM8K accuracy (last boxed answer), mean tokens, and the rank correlation across arms of predicted cost with held-out non-loop damage. No arm, layer, or coefficient is chosen using test-roster outcomes. All arms are reported including failures.

Decision rule: an analytic arm counts as an improvement only if its loop-fraction interval excludes zero, its accuracy interval does not exclude a loss, and it beats both the random-direction and random-support controls on the same metric. Otherwise the result is reported as negative.
