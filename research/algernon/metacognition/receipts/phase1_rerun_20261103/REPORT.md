## Reading
The earlier claim "learned controller loses cost-adjusted utility to the two-step heuristic" is reproduced for H-M1 (confirmation range) and as a tie for H-M0. The H-M0 terminal policy improves cost, not utility. Why the learned policy trails (distillation gap to the exact planner, 8.5k parameters, no persistent hypothesis state beyond the featurized belief) is a hypothesis, not yet tested: Phase 2 ablations (cost-aware stop head, VOI estimator, recurrent state, matched FF) are the planned test.

H-M0-01 (pre-terminal probe selector) did not improve correctness over fixed_heuristic (-0.028) or bayes_ig (-0.037); it is the weak predecessor.
