# DISTILL-15: Lazy bounded certificate memory

DISTILL-14 established good learned retrieval recall but negative total economics, including the precomputed bank. The next test starts with empty caches and admits only results from computations requested in a stream. No trained model participates.

## Frozen design

36 new roots (12 ordinary, 12 shifted, 12 scarce), two paired streams of 128 nonterminal queries per root, capacities 8,32,128. The distinct stream samples unique public literal states uniformly from complete reachable closure, stages 1-3, without equivalence labels. The rollout stream samples weighted worlds at the referee and records incumbent-reachable public states in stages 1-3 until 128 queries. It represents repeated solves across independent public trajectories under one spec; it is not an online end-to-end policy deployment. Repeats are expected; report unique literal states and quotient classes, and reuse of a different literal state/mask.

Seven arms: cold exact independent solve with per-query literal memo; root-only persistent literal LRU; root-only persistent normalized quotient LRU; recursive persistent literal LRU; recursive persistent quotient LRU; stage hybrid recursive LRU (literal stages 0-1, marginal 2-3); selective hybrid (same keys; admit only after the realized solve costs at least twice key acquisition plus 32 logical units). All persistent stores begin empty, are root/stream/arm scoped, and obey the capacity. Local literal memo is common to every arm; report its peak separately. Memo hit values are exact integer unnormalized sums divided by the signature mass factor on insertion, and multiplied by the new factor on lookup. The incumbent policy is fixed. Each returned pair is checked against an independent exact Auditor, including risk. No learned cancellation, cross-root reuse, hidden world access in the solver, or training admission.

Account for key construction and lookup, normalized value storage/retrieval, LRU eviction, selective admission check, exact selection, reward/transition evaluation and local memo. No bank setup is excluded. Reference values and query stream generation are referee work common to all arms. Process CPU descriptive only; logical work is the primary endpoint. Report prefix work, per-root savings, capacities, hits, different-state and different-mask hits, expansions, evictions, and incorrect reuse count. Preserve all negative results. No native model updates or provider calls.
