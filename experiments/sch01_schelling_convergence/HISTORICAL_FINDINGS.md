# SCH-01B: verified historical anchors and unresolved pathways

*Source review dated 2026-10-30. This ledger distinguishes textual/documentary evidence, scholars' historical interpretation, and unresolved hypotheses. Entries refer to accessible abstracts/entries, not paywalled full books.*

## Directly attested routes

**Greek Plotinus to Arabic Plotinus.** The Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy's [*The Theology of Aristotle*](https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/spr2019/entries/theology-aristotle/) dates the Arabic Plotinus materials to the ninth century and associates production with al-Kindi's circle. The text transmits reworked parts of the *Enneads* under Aristotle's name. A second [SEP entry on Greek sources](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/arabic-islamic-greek/) describes this transmission within Islamic philosophy. The Arabic material is an interpretation and adaptation, so equivalence with original Plotinian ontology must be argued passage by passage.

**Christian scripture to the modern Bible Student movement.** The Witnesses' [official history](https://www.jw.org/en/library/brochures/Jehovahs-Witnesses-Who-Are-They-What-Do-They-Believe/Their-Modern-Development-and-Growth/) identifies a Bible-study group near Pittsburgh in the early 1870s and the launch of *Zion's Watch Tower* in July 1879. The organization's [founder FAQ](https://www.jw.org/en/jehovahs-witnesses/faq/founder/) describes its scriptural methodology and continuity to present Witnesses. Its current [beliefs summary](https://www.jw.org/en/jehovahs-witnesses/faq/jehovah-witness-beliefs/) describes divine government, future resurrection and enduring earthly life. This is institution-level doctrinal inheritance and does not establish a specific modern claim about Kurzweil.

**American AI eschatology and Indian reinterpretation.** Geraci's [*Futures of Artificial Intelligence*](https://academic.oup.com/book/41826), published February 22, 2022, directly discusses the reception and adaptation of American transhumanist thinking in Indian scientific and cultural settings. It offers evidence for broad cross-cultural movement and distinguishes Indian reinterpretation. It does not establish Kurzweil's direct reading of Śaṅkara or Daoist authors.

## Important prior art

Geraci's [2008 *Journal of the American Academy of Religion* article](https://doi.org/10.1093/jaarel/lfm101) identifies apocalyptic features in AI futurism. His [2010 *Apocalyptic AI*](https://academic.oup.com/book/11442) explicitly names Kurzweil and Moravec, while treating Christian and Jewish apocalyptic imagery as a significant influence on technological futurism. The publisher's [chapter abstract](https://academic.oup.com/book/11442/chapter-abstract/160128670) connects this expression to science fiction. These are a documented scholarly genealogy of an idea, not proof of Kurzweil's individual biblical reading history.

Geraci's [2018 *Temples of Modernity*](https://books.google.com/books?id=VUphEQAAQBAJ) examined transhumanism alongside Hindu practices in South Indian scientific communities. The 2022 book extends that study to American and Indian AI futures. SCH-01's originality, if demonstrated, lies in disciplined *causality-preserving comparison and information-loss measurement* rather than the original observation that eschatology and AI narratives resemble one another.

Kurzweil's [2005 Q&A](https://www.writingsbyraykurzweil.com/a-singularity-q-a) presents a forecast of a 2045 singularity with recursive nonbiological redesign. The site repost date is 2024; the text itself is identified there as from 2005. Jehovah's Witness resurrection means divine restoration of a deceased individual, not ordinary postbiological emulation.

## Major epistemic seams

- **Witnesses → Kurzweil:** no direct source or verified exposure pathway has been identified in this bounded search. A generic Jewish/Christian cultural inheritance is a different hypothesis from the specific Jehovah's Witness inheritance claim.
- **Śaṅkara/Daoism/Plotinus → Kurzweil:** chronological possibility and conceptual resemblance cannot establish that Kurzweil encountered a specific passage. Check bibliographies, interviews and intermediate authors before promoting these to edges.
- **Plotinus → Islamic theology:** Greek philosophical works demonstrably entered Arabic-speaking philosophy. Direct identification with Muʿtazilī tawḥīd, and any specific Muʿtazilī author's use of a translation, demands more detailed primary evidence.
- **Indian traditions → modern Indian AI visions:** the Geraci studies support broad reception and adaptation at a social level. The existence of local Hindu vocabulary in an AI proposal does not independently prove ancient convergence.

## Evidence-grade decision

We have a proof of *semantic information loss* for a chosen finite quotient, plus a sourced historical graph. This is enough to challenge facile claims of independence or identity between traditions. The passage-level empirical and theological questions remain open. A future result can demonstrate robust cross-cultural structural agreement only after it survives blinded independent coding, source-level scrutiny, and appropriate negative controls.
